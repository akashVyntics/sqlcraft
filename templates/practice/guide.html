How to practice

  1. Read the business definition, output aliases, NULL rules and tie breakers.
  2. Inspect the public schema and rows.
  3. Run on public data, then submit against three datasets.
  4. Use a hint before revealing the solution; try again from a blank editor.
  5. Review your submission history and continue with an unsolved question.
